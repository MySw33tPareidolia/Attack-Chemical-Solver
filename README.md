C++ tool I made to instantly complete the chemical step on **Attack Of The Radioactive Thing**. Instead of doing the math.


### 🧪 Chemical Solver

1. Enter your M-value (Found in the Shed by the pool).
2. Find your multiple by using the **Chart Produced By The Program** and comparing it to the TV next to Elvira.
3. Change the Color to your color.
4. Enter your Acetaldehyde Diamond Values from Left to Top.
5. Finally Choose what Chemically you would like to build!
